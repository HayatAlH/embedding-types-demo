3 · Which embedding is best? Scored over all 240 designs

Maps: t-SNE (perplexity 30) of each embedding, a 2-D view of a higher-dimensional space, so distances are approximate. Synthetic designs: r(θ) = s·(1 + a·cos k(θ − φ)) with k = 3–6 lobes (60 each), lobe depth a, size s, rotation φ. All three networks see the same input, the outline sampled at 64 angles. Autoencoder 64→64→8→64→64 (MSE); classifier 64→64→16→4 (embedding = its 16-number layer); contrastive 64→64→16 with a triplet loss on randomly rotated and resized copies, never shown lobe labels. Neighbours by Euclidean distance. “Kept / ignored” compares how much the 5 neighbours differ from the chosen design versus random pairs, averaged over all designs. Footprint = pixel intersection-over-union at 32×32.
